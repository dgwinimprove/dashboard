📝 STAR STORIES — ANSWERING BEHAVIORAL QUESTIONS

What is STAR? It's a framework for answering "Tell me about a time when..." interview questions. Each story has 4 parts:
S
Situation: Set the scene
T
Task: What needed to happen
A
Action: What YOU did
R
Result: The outcome
Write these out fully. Memorize the structure, not the exact words. Speak naturally.
Story 1: Problem Solved Under Pressure
S — Delaware North, 50,000 attendee event, POS system crashed
T — Guests couldn't check out, long lines forming, event reputation at risk
A — Stayed calm, switched to manual processing, coordinated with team, kept guests informed
R — Got everyone checked out, event ended smoothly, supervisor praised composure
Story 2: Leadership / Initiative
S — Maximus Federal, working in a team where no one was stepping up
T — Needed to improve team performance and workflow
A — Took initiative to organize the team, created structure, mentored peers
R — First person promoted to Mentor role, team performance improved
Story 3: Working Under High Volume / Deadlines
S — Tax Defense Network, managing 250+ clients with high-stakes deadlines
T — Needed to handle massive volume accurately under pressure
A — Organized workflow, prioritized deadlines, maintained accuracy under stress
R — Met all deadlines, maintained quality, built reputation for reliability
💡 For R1: They care about customer service, accuracy, multi-system work, and staying calm under pressure. Your STAR stories hit all of these.
